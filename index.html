    Movement

    CPMA physics. Strafe-jump (hold W + A/D, steer with the mouse, keep jumping) to build speed. With W alone in the air you get CPM air control for sharp turns; A/D alone gives fast air strafing. Hold Jump to bunny hop on landing. On ramps your jump carries their upward speed. Rooms can turn on the CPM double jump (a second jump within 0.4 s, for stairs and ledges).

    Walljump (UT style). While airborne right next to a wall, double-tap a direction pointing away from it, or just press Jump, to kick off the wall. It's the only way to jump again in the air: one walljump per jump, it needs a real wall beside you, and there's no ground dodge.
